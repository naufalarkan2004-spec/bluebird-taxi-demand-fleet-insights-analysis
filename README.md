<h1> Blue Bird: Trip-Rate Crisis &amp; Fleet Gap </h1>

> Data Analytics Capstone Project: taxi fleet productivity and corporate eVoucher control (data from October–December 2023).

## 1. Project Overview
Blue Bird is a conventional taxi operator with a physical fleet and permanent drivers, so vehicles and drivers are **fixed costs**. Every hour on duty without a passenger is cost without revenue. Meanwhile, the Finance teams of several corporate clients have questioned the transparency of **eVoucher** usage in the *Corporate Solution* service.

This project analyzes six operational tables (trips, requests, shifts, drivers, pools, corporate clients) to answer one big question: **why are many Blue Bird drivers idle while some customers cannot get a taxi?** The business focus is twofold: **Fleet Optimization** (trip rate and idle time) and **B2B Fraud Mitigation** (eVoucher usage patterns).

Key Objectives:
- Objective 1: Ensure the data is reliable through cleaning and validation (service categories, taximeter data, keys and relationships between tables) without altering the original data.
- Objective 2: Measure fleet productivity (trips per shift, idle time, utilization) and understand disparities across pools and zones.
- Objective 3: Identify when and where customer requests go unfulfilled.
- Objective 4: Screen for eVoucher usage patterns that need verification, then formulate measurable operational and control recommendations.

## 2. Data Sources
Data is loaded from the project's internal MySQL database (credentials are read from environment variables and are not written in the notebook). Time coverage: **October–December 2023**.

| Table | Contents | Primary key |
|---|---|---|
| `bb_trips` | Completed trips: time, distance, fare, service, payment (~318K raw rows) | `trip_id` |
| `bb_service_requests` | Customer requests and their status, fulfilled or failed (289,622 rows) | `request_id` |
| `bb_driver_shifts` | Driver shifts: clock-in/out, trip count, idle time (252,524 rows) | `shift_id` |
| `bb_drivers` | Driver attributes | `driver_id` |
| `bb_pools` | Pools (garages): region and zone affinity | `pool_id` |
| `bb_corporate_clients` | Corporate clients and their industries | `company_id` |

Main relationships: `pools → drivers → shifts → trips`, `corporate_clients → trips`, and `trips → service_requests` (via `matched_trip_id`).

## 5. Summary of Finding
### 5.1 Business Insight

> All findings are **associative** (three months of observational data). The eVoucher pattern is a signal to verify, not proof of misuse.

| Key finding | Key figures |
|---|---|
| **Failed requests are concentrated in the CBD at peak hours** | Overall, 93.30% of requests are fulfilled and 6.70% fail (19,397 of 289,622). CBD at 07:00–08:59 and 17:00–18:59 fails **36.6%** (5.5% at other hours in the same zone). These four hours are only 14.3% of requests but 45.6% of failures. |
| **Busy does not mean failing** | The busiest hours (12:00–16:59) fail only 4.08%. Waiting time is flat (median 5.0 minutes), so a failure means a lost request, not a longer wait. |
| **Drivers are very idle** | On average **1.26 trips per shift**, **30.6% of shifts with no trips**, **9.4%** utilization, and only 0.35% of shifts reach 6 trips. |
| **The strongest cause of idleness is location, not pool** | Zone explains 9.7% of the variation in trips per shift. Airport has 21.8% of shifts but only 12.6% of requests (51% of Airport shifts have no trips, vs 19% in the CBD). After controlling for zone, pool adds nothing (p = 0.62). |
| **Low trip rate, but no proven decline** | No trend over 12 full weeks; the decline mentioned in the brief cannot be confirmed. |
| **The Airport eVoucher pattern needs verification** | **65.9%** of Airport eVoucher trips occur at night or on weekends, vs **22.6%** for Non-B2B trips, uniform across 200 companies. |
| **eVoucher by pickup zone shows no sign of fraud** | 66.4% of eVoucher trips are picked up outside the CBD, almost the same as other customers (61.7%). Fares are not more often high and no company stands out. The CBD-only rule is unconfirmed and destination zone is unavailable. |
| **Meter data quality affects the numbers** | After excluding 7,091 trips, 0.38% of trips with unreliable distance still inflate the average distance by about 18%. |

### 5.2 Actionable Recommendation

KPI baselines come from this data, while target values are set by management. All recommendations are proposals for trials or verification, not causal conclusions.

| # | Recommendation | KPI (baseline) | Priority | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pilot clock-in/clock-out scheduling** so more drivers are in the CBD at 07:00–08:59 and 17:00–18:59, with a control group | CBD failure rate in those four hours (36.6%); drivers on duty per hour | **High** | Operations |
| 2 | **Verify weekend/night Airport eVoucher trips**: request client travel policies, require a trip reason, review a random sample; confirm the CBD zone rule and request destination zone data | Share of weekend/night Airport eVoucher trips (65.9%) | **High** | Finance |
| 3 | **Pilot moving some Airport shifts (especially PL-CGK) to the CBD** at peak hours | Shifts with no trips (30.6%; Airport 51.1%); trips per shift (1.26) | Medium | Operations |
| 4 | **Set a baseline and target for trip rate**, monitor weekly by pool and zone; do not use the 6-trip flag as a KPI | Trips per driver-day (1.26); idle share (~90%) | Medium | Operations |
| 5 | **Evaluate pools by zone-adjusted trip rate** | Gap in trips per shift versus each pool's expected value | Medium | Operations |
| 6 | **Review the number of afternoon shifts against afternoon demand** | Afternoon trips per shift (1.01 vs 1.31 morning) | Low–Medium | Operations |
| 7 | **Add meter data validation** at input and require a booking reference for eVoucher use | 2.2% of trips excluded; 0.38% unreliable distance | Low–Medium | Finance |

**Limitations:** only three months of data; results are associative; failed requests carry no driver or company information and Street Hail demand is not visible; the definition of "unusual time" and the 500 km / 1 km distance thresholds are analysis assumptions; the 6-trip target in `ritase_target_met` is not defined in the brief.
